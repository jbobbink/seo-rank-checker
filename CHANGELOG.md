# Changelog

Users of the plugin only receive changes when the `version` in `.claude-plugin/plugin.json` is bumped, so every entry here corresponds to one bump.

## 1.0.0 (2026-11-17)

- Initial release, connected to the Rank Checker endpoint `https://mcp.gscwizard.com/mcp/rank-checker` and its nine tools: `list_sites`, `get_account_info`, `list_rank_keyword_sets`, `get_rank_keyword_data`, `add_rank_keywords`, `remove_rank_keywords`, `get_ranking_changes`, `query_top_queries` and `get_query_performance`.
- Four guided skills: track-keywords, rank-report, rank-drops and prune-keywords.
